# Telugu States – Expanded Services

Adds department-specific official service portal cards for Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, in addition to the state-wide AP Seva and Telangana MeeSeva cards already present.

Andhra Pradesh additions include land records, registration, grievance redressal, Grama/Ward Sachivalayam, municipal services, APSRTC, transport, police, health, agriculture, APPSC, school and intermediate boards, finance, energy, electricity distribution, social welfare, women/child welfare and skill development.

Telangana additions include Bhu Bharati, Registration & Stamps, Telangana Police, Hyderabad Police, GHMC, eMunicipal, Transport, TGSRTC, Health, Aarogyasri, Employees Health Scheme, Agriculture, ePASS, school/intermediate boards, SCERT, Aasara, Stree Nidhi, BC Welfare, Women/Child Welfare, Employment & Training, Treasury, power sector, building permissions, birth/death registration, Tourism and eGazette.

Telangana URLs are drawn from the official Telangana State Web Directory and State Services pages. Andhra Pradesh links are drawn from official state department/district resources and government directories. Each portal is a link to the responsible department; exact online functions and eligibility are determined by that portal.
