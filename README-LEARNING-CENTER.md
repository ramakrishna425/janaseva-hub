# Janaseva Hub – Learning Center update

This update adds a **Learning Center** to the existing Janaseva Hub project. It is a curated directory of external learning resources; it does not download or republish third-party books, videos, images or PDFs.

## Included in the Learning Center

- Anganwadi / Early Childhood Care and Education (birth–6 years): Navchetana, Aadharshila, activity calendars, ECCE toolkits and Telugu resources.
- School (Classes 1–10): DIKSHA, ePathshala, NCERT, Andhra Pradesh school textbooks/workbooks, Telangana SCERT, CBSE and NIOS.
- Intermediate (1st and 2nd year): AP and Telangana Intermediate Board portals and senior-secondary DIKSHA resources.
- Graduation and higher education: SWAYAM, NPTEL, e-PG Pathshala, CEC and National Digital Library of India.
- Competitive examinations: UPSC previous papers, SSC syllabus, RRB, APPSC, TGPSC and NTA.
- Health, physical activity and inclusion: Manodarpan, Fit India, NCERT and DIKSHA accessible learning.
- Parent/caregiver support, plus simple offline play/activity ideas and guidance on recognizing and responding to child stress.

## How to use

1. Open **Learning Center** in the main navigation.
2. Choose a learning stage (Anganwadi, School, Intermediate, Graduation, Competitive Exams, Well-being or Parents & Teachers).
3. Choose a resource format (PDFs/books, videos, pictures/diagrams, activities, courses, papers or support).
4. Search by topic, class, subject, exam or publisher.
5. Select **Open resource** to visit the publisher's website in a new tab.

## Important content and safety notes

- The links point to the respective publishers' official or institutional resource portals. Some portals may require selecting a class, subject, medium, board or course after opening.
- The Learning Center is a directory, not a mirror or download host. Copyright, licensing, registration and access terms are controlled by each publisher.
- Not every resource is available in every language or for every State Board. Confirm the current syllabus and academic year on the relevant board's official site.
- For children, activities must be age-appropriate, inclusive and supervised by a responsible adult. Avoid choking hazards, unsafe equipment and forced exercise. Do not use screens as a substitute for sleep, conversation, movement or play.
- The child stress information is general educational guidance, not a diagnosis or treatment. Persistent or severe distress should be discussed with a qualified health professional or school counsellor.
- External links can change. If a link is unavailable, use the publisher's main website to locate the latest resource.
