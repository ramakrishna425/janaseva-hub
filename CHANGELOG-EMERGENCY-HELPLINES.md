# Emergency Helpline Directory

- Added Emergency Helplines under Tools & Resources.
- Added national helplines based on the National Portal of India helpline directory.
- Added 112 ERSS contacts for all 28 States and 8 Union Territories. The Ministry of Home Affairs states ERSS 112 is operational across all 36 States/UTs.
- State/district-specific direct control-room numbers are intentionally not guessed; verify local numbers with the relevant official government portal.
- Sources: https://www.india.gov.in/directory/helpline and https://www.mha.gov.in/emergency-response-support-system-erss
