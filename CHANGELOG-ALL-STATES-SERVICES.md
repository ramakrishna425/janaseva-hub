# All States & Union Territories Services update

- Added citizen-service portal cards for all 28 States and 8 Union Territories.
- Kept existing Central Government service cards.
- Changed the category filter label to “All State & UT services”.
- Preserved the existing Live Updates endpoint and backend files.
- Portal names and URLs are based on the Department of Administrative Reforms and Public Grievances (DARPG), NeSDA Way Forward April 2026 report. Mizoram and Lakshadweep were reported as not having a single unified portal in that report; their official administration websites are linked instead.
